# Code of Conduct

The CodeVerse Hub is a community for people learning, building, and shipping.
We want a welcoming space for beginners and experts.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior:

- Be respectful and kind, even during disagreements
- Assume good intent; ask clarifying questions
- Give constructive, actionable feedback
- Be patient with newcomers and help them learn

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing someone’s private information (“doxxing”)
- Encouraging others to do any of the above

## Scope

This applies to all CodeVerse Hub spaces:

- GitHub repos, issues, PRs, discussions, and review comments
- Community chat spaces where org work is coordinated
- Project events or meetups (online or in-person)

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness a violation:

- Report it by emailing: **contact@thecodeversehub.tech**
- Include links, screenshots, and timestamps if possible

The maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including:

- Warning the person
- Removing content
- Temporarily or permanently banning accounts from org spaces

## Appeal

If you believe an enforcement action was incorrect, reply to the enforcement email
with additional context.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by common open-source community standards.
